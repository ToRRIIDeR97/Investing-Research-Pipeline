## 20. Risks and catalysts

#### Risks:
- **Credit cycle deterioration and loan losses** (Severity: 5): SoFi lends through Lending and Financial Services segments. A downturn may increase member default rates, reduce demand for loans, raise charge-offs and provisions, and reduce net income. The company discloses it operates in a cyclical industry, and its Lending and Financial Services segments may be particularly negatively impacted by worsening economic conditions.
- **Deposit funding cost and liquidity pressure** (Severity: 4): SoFi's funding mix shifted primarily to deposits after becoming a bank holding company. An economic downturn or deposit competition could cause outflows, lower checking and savings balances, shift funds to perceived safer alternatives, increase funding costs, and force greater reliance on wholesale funding. That would compress net interest income and net margin.
- **Capital markets and gain-on-sale dependence** (Severity: 4): SoFi uses a gain-on-sale origination model and states its earnings and financial condition are largely dependent on loan sale prices in capital markets. Historically it funded operations through equity, loan sales, secured and unsecured borrowing facilities, and securitizations. The Loan Platform Business is new and lacks significant performance history. A closed or expensive loan sale market could reduce noninterest income and force balance-sheet retention.
- **Growth deceleration in members and products** (Severity: 3): SoFi has grown members and products rapidly, but if customer acquisition slows or cross-sell weakens, revenue growth and operating leverage decline. Q1 2026 had 14.7 million members and 22.2 million products; Q2 2026 had 15.8 million members and 24.4 million products. A slowdown would pressure the terminal revenue and net income path embedded in the base valuation.
- **Regulatory capital constraints and dilution** (Severity: 4): SoFi became a bank holding company and operates SoFi Bank, N.A., so it is subject to regulatory capital requirements. Growth, credit stress, or regulatory changes could require capital raises, diluting shareholders beyond the reported diluted share count and impairing per-share value. Tier 1 capital ratio was 14.3% as of Q3 2023, but current regulatory capital details are not in the supplied latest metrics.
- **Capital allocation errors from limited segment-level profitability visibility** (Severity: 3): SoFi operates three reportable segments: Lending, Technology Platform, and Financial Services. If internal and external disclosure of segment-level contribution profit is delayed or incomplete, management may be slower to reallocate capital and expenses away from underperforming segments; investors may also misprice each segment. This can cause continued investment in low-return areas, delayed cost actions, and lower consolidated ROE than the base case assumes.
