Pick a company: one search bar

Step 1 of Start research. The two cards ("Pick a company" and "Add a listed company") become a single bar. It searches your companies and Yahoo Finance together, so a new listing is one click: picking a Yahoo result adds the company and opens the question step.

The bar is live: type a name or ticker (try nvidia, sofi, bank, asml), use ↑ ↓ and Enter. Results are sample Yahoo data.

How search works

A new server route proxies Yahoo's search endpoint (the browser never calls Yahoo), debounced at ~250 ms, equities and ETFs only. Your companies match locally first, so they appear instantly.

Picking a Yahoo result

Adds the company with Yahoo's name and symbol (no form to fill), then opens the question step. A symbol you already have goes to that company instead of making a duplicate.

When Yahoo is down

Your companies still search. Below them, "Add SYMBOL anyway" keeps today's manual path for a ticker Yahoo can't find.
