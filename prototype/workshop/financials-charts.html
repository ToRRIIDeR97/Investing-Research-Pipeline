SoFi's real quarterly figures, USD millions. Pick a chart builder (how you add line items and bundles) and a table style (how statements read). A bundle is a named set of line items you chart together, like "Revenue, cash flow and expenses". It follows you to every company, and can be drawn as separate series, stacked, or summed into one line.
