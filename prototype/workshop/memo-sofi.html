Why

Three reasons the price isn't supported

Each figure links to its source. Click a number chip to see where it came from.

Growth is using up capital and shares

Loans rose from $38.0bn to $53.7bn in nine months. That pushed the holding company's core capital ratio down from 22.8% to 16.9%. Shares outstanding are up 7.6% in a year, and stock pay equals 39% of net income.

22.8% → 16.9%core capital ratio, 9 months

Profit isn't turning into cash

Net income over the last twelve months was $738m, but free cash flow was −$8.8bn. The gap is mostly loans originated for sale faster than they were sold. If those sales catch up this could reverse, but it hasn't yet.

−$8.8bnfree cash flow, last 12 months

Returns look below what shareholders require

SoFi earns roughly 8.7% on its invested capital, against a 13.1% cost of equity. The gap is indicative: the cost of equity rests on a beta that hasn't been verified unverified.

8.7% vs 13.1%return vs required return

The other side

  • Capital is well above the minimums. The bank's ratio is 14.9% against a 6.5% "well capitalised" level, and $15.9bn of liquidity is available.
  • Credit is improving: total net charge-offs fell from 2.63% in 2024 to 1.62% in Q3 2026.
  • Net interest income grew 52% year on year in Q2 2026, at a 5.98% margin.
Plain English, capped at three. The current report opens with 12 dense paragraphs ("on the packet's bank-capital-constrained cash-flow method…"). Here each reason is one claim, one number and a link to its source. The full chapters move to Audit.

How we got $1.13

The valuation in one picture

Bank valuation: the cash shareholders can take out after the bank keeps the capital it needs, discounted at 13.1% a year.

Why today's value is far below the 2030 targets

The base case's end-2030 price target is $9.36, but today's value is only $1.13. To keep growing loans, SoFi has to keep back more capital than it earns until 2030. That shortfall, about $5.3bn in today's money, comes off the value before any 2030 payoff.

So the two numbers answer different questions. $9.36 is what the stock might trade at in 2030. $1.13 is what that path is worth to you now.

Open questions

What the research couldn't establish

These are why confidence is low. Re-running research targets them first.

  • Is SoFi's deposit cost actually below market? Only a comparison against its own warehouse lines (156 bp) was found; no market benchmark.blocks a signal
  • Does Galileo/Technisys make money after corporate costs? Contribution fell from $47.9m to $11.8m a quarter, and reported revenue figures don't reconcile.blocks a signal
  • Bank-level capital and risk-weighted assets by quarter, needed to test the capital path.weakens valuation
  • Q3 2026 operating income, capex and working capital, needed to confirm the cash-flow inputs.weakens valuation
Gaps are separated from the thesis. Items like "beta is verified" and "Q3 operating income is supplied" currently sit in "Must be true" next to real business conditions. They are research to-dos, so they live here.
