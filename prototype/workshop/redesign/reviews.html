On tablets and phones the form sits below the findings and a navy "Decide" bar stays pinned to the bottom of the screen. Shown mid-choice: "Approve for tracking" is selected and the acknowledgement appears. Before a choice, no option is selected and the checkbox is hidden. Allowed choices come from the same checks the server runs (review gate and withheld conclusion); the queue returns which approvals are allowed. If the research inputs changed since the report was built, approval is refused and the form says "Regenerate the report first".

What goes away: the 8-tile number strip, run IDs, the made-up "Medium" priority, the research brief at the top (it's on the run page), the risk ranking (it's in the memo), the full report printed below the findings, and a verdict menu that started on "Approved for tracking". Wording: "needs more work" now means only your verdict; the AI's outcome is "passed with conditions" or "didn't pass".
