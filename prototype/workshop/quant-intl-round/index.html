      6 · PragValue and PragTrend descriptions

      Today each is one sentence on the finder and nothing on the ticker page.

      D1What · How · How to readfinder cards in three short parts with key terms; the ticker page gets the same text behind an "About PragValue" link in each tab's first card

      PragValue

      What it is

      An estimate of what one share is worth, in three cases (bear, base, bull), and a grade for how cheap the stock is against that.

      How it's built

      The company is routed to one of eight valuation engines by what it does (banks on book value and returns, miners across the cycle, growers on sales). Each engine runs a five-year DCF on its metrics, ending at exit multiples from the company's own history, and takes the weighted median.

      How to read it

      The tiles show each case's value against today's price. PQ Grade (A to F) weighs that gap with how much it could gain against how much it could lose; PulseScore runs −3 (expensive) to +3 (cheap).

      DCFExit multiplePQ GradePulseScore

      PragTrend

      What it is

      Whether the price is in a healthy uptrend, from daily closes only, measured against the market's index.

      How it's built

      Nine trend-template rules (price above its 50, 150 and 200-day averages, the 200-day rising, near its high), the Weinstein stage from the 30-week average, and relative strength against the S&P 500 (or the local index abroad) and the sector fund.

      How to read it

      More rules met is stronger; stage 2 is advancing, stage 4 declining; an RS rating above 70 means it beat most of the market over the year.
